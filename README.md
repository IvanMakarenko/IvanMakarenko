# Hi there 👋

My name is Ivan Makarenko — **Solution Architect (SaaS / Enterprise)** from Kharkiv, Ukraine.

I design extensible SaaS platforms, embeddable widget ecosystems, and modular data-driven products. My core belief: **complexity should be absorbed by architecture, not by users or developers** — so products evolve through composition, not rewrites.

Currently I architect and own acquisition & retention products end-to-end at [CASAFARI](https://www.casafari.com) (EU proptech): an embeddable lead-generation widget platform with SaaS orchestration (tenant-level configuration, CRM callbacks, paywalled add-ons), interactive map-based data products, and no-code landing generators. I also own the **integration & partner engineering** side — the technical layer that makes cross-sell and reseller deals possible.

### 🔌 Integrations & Partner Engineering

I close the gap between "we signed a partnership" and "it actually works in production":

- **Reseller / whitelabel channels** — onboarded a 2,000+ widget reseller integration in days, not weeks, because the platform's extension model absorbed a new distribution channel without architectural changes
- **CRM-level integrations** — bidirectional lead delivery into partner CRM ecosystems, enabling cross-sell motions for the business
- **Integration contracts as products** — versioned partner APIs with idempotent bulk operations, webhook callbacks, and tenant-scoped configuration, designed so the next partner costs near-zero engineering

### 🧠 Design Mindset

- Product simplicity over technical complexity
- Architecture should disappear behind the product experience
- Extension over rewrite: new capabilities without breaking existing contracts
